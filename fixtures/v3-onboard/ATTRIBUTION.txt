V3 Ventures onboard seed corpus — ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY
====================================================

This snapshot is for Venture OS smoke / handoff testing. It is NOT the live V3
book and must not be used for investment decisions.

Sources (public):
- 16-company illustrative operating book: vendored from the V3 Agentic OS demo
  bundle at https://v3.heisenbug.in/_next/static/chunks/651-f14569331a86d75b.js
  (originally lib/data/corpus.json in the private demo repo). The demo landing
  page states operating figures are illustrative.
- 20 additional portfolio company profiles: names + websites (+ sector where
  listed) scraped from https://www.v3.ventures (public portfolio links only).

What is illustrative:
- Monthly MIS extracts, ownership %, cost basis, marks/NAV inputs, inbox
  proposals, and FX samples in the 16-company book are a realistic working set
  invented for workflow testing.
- Public-profile companies have NO invented metrics, ownership, NAV, or inbox.
- Missing fields are intentionally omitted (missing ≠ 0).
