# Competitive notes

Venture OS is the **investment-team book**. Adjacent products solve different jobs. Do not clone their UX or collapse their meanings of “data room”.

---

## 1. Visible

- Job: founder-friendly metric collection + LP-facing updates.
- Strength: gets *some* numbers in without an analyst.
- Gap for V3: founders will not re-key non-standard MIS; V3 already has OneDrive packs. Visible-style portals do not solve unit chaos, restatements, or dual INR crore + EUR with an FX audit trail.
- We do **not** become a founder portal in v1.

## 2. Standard Metrics

- Job: institutional portfolio monitoring, document parse, **peer benchmarks**.
- Strength: scale + network dataset.
- Gap: benchmark fill must never silently replace a missing V3 figure. Their AI parse still needs HITL for a 15–40 name book where headline error is unacceptable.
- We parse into **our** firm schema and stop at the inbox.

## 3. Affinity

- Job: relationship CRM / deal graph.
- Strength: who spoke to whom.
- Gap: not a book. Notes are subjective. We integrate later; we do not recreate CRM.
- v1: connector **not connected**.

## 4. Carta

- Job: cap table, fund admin, 409A-adjacent marks as a *legal* system of record.
- Strength: equity truth for lawyers and LPs.
- Gap: not MIS, not inbox, not Ask over board packs.
- Our marks are **investment-team** marks (method + rationale + source). We do not replace Carta.

---

## 5. Three meanings of “data room”

| Meaning | Who enters | What leaks if confused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Company vault** | Analysts / partners | Mixing LP-ready and working files |
| **Firm library** | Partners / ops | Templates treated as portco evidence |
| **LP / ILPA room** | LPs (Phase 2) | Internal commentary shipping to LPs |

HITL confirm is the control that stops vault extracts from becoming LP facts.

## 6. HITL confirm (our wedge)

Visible and Standard Metrics both move toward auto-post. V3’s brief is the opposite: **90%+ auto-ingest into inbox**, near-zero headline error. The product should feel like a dealing room, not an AI inbox that “already updated the dashboard”.
